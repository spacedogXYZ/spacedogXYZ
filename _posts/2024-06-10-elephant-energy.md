After two great years at IO, I left to join a friend's climate-tech startup based in Denver. Elephant Energy has helped thousands of homeowners electrify their biggest investment; installing heat pumps, EV chargers, and super-efficient water heaters. As Founding Engineer, I will help accelerate their progress and build software to scale with their ambitions.

--

Update March 2026

Unfortunately the startup world is a fickle one, and my time at Elephant Energy came to a close after 18 months. This was a good lesson in the challenges of working in the real world under a changing regulatory environment. When the IRS federal tax credits for heat pumps and solar panels expired at the end of 2025, we had to adjust our strategy to ensure that our customers were eligible for all local, state and utility incentives. 

I am proud of the work the team and I did to help homeowners understand the cost of a heatpump in their home.  I developed a detailed energy model with a simple interface, validated it against actual utility usage, and ensured it met the needs of both customers and our sales process. I helped other engineers improve the performance of their queries, validate code correctness, and simplified APIs across the stack.

I am now looking for my next challenge in the climate tech or political advocacy world. Watch this space for further updates!
